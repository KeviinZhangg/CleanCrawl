Governments worldwide are drafting AI regulation frameworks. This article covers key proposals from the EU, US, and UK. Transparency requirements, safety testing, and liability rules are central themes. Companies must prepare for compliance while continuing innovation in artificial intelligence systems and applications.

The regulatory landscape continues to evolve with new bills introduced each quarter. Stakeholders include technology companies, civil society groups, and academic researchers who contribute to policy discussions and public consultations on AI governance frameworks globally.
